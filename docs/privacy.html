Privacy Policy

Bolton Walking and Outdoors Appreciation Society (BWOAS) app • Last updated 30 July 2026

This app is run by Bolton Walking and Outdoors Appreciation Society, a volunteer-run community walking group. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects almost nothing, doesn't sell or share anything, and doesn't show ads.

Location

The Routes tab can show your current position on the map, so you can see it alongside the club's walking routes. This uses your device's location purely to display it on screen - it is never sent to us, stored, or shared with anyone. You can decline the location permission and the map will still work, just without the "my location" marker.

App content (routes, events, committee)

Walking routes, scheduled walks, and committee member information shown in the app are stored in a Google Firebase database and downloaded by the app so you can view them. This is the club's own public content - it is not personal data about you, and viewing it does not require you to sign in or create an account.

Downloading route files

If you download a GPX route file or risk assessment PDF from the Routes tab, your device fetches that file directly and saves it wherever you choose (using your phone's normal save/share screen). No record of what you've downloaded is sent to us.

Booking a walk

Tapping "Book this walk" or "Open full Ticket Tailor calendar" opens our booking page on Ticket Tailor in your browser. Any information you enter there (name, email, payment details) is handled by Ticket Tailor under their own privacy policy, not this one - we don't receive or see anything you enter unless you'd have told us anyway (e.g. your name on a booking).

Links to our website and email

The About tab has buttons to email the club or visit boltonwalking.co.uk. These open your device's own mail app or browser - we don't track when you tap them.

What we don't do

  • We don't require you to create an account or sign in to use the app.
  • We don't collect your name, email, or contact details through the app itself.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking to monitor how you use the app.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell or share any data with third parties for marketing.

Services we use

The app is built on Google's Firebase and Google Maps platforms, which provide the map display and the database behind the app's content. Google's own privacy policy covers how they handle data on their end: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

This app isn't directed at children and isn't designed to collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles information changes, we'll update this page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
